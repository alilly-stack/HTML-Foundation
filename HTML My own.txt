            <h2>Milestones in Modern Astronomy</h2>
            <p>Advancements in space telescopes and planetary probes have unlocked incredible mysteries over the past few decades.</p>

            <article>
                <h3>Exoplanet Detection</h3>
                <p>Astronomers have confirmed the existence of thousands of planets orbiting stars outside our solar system, many of which reside in habitable zones where liquid water could exist.</p>
            </article>

            <article>
                <h3>Gravitational Waves</h3>
                <p>First observed in 2015, gravitational waves—ripples in spacetime caused by colliding black holes—opened an entirely new way to "hear" cosmic events.</p>
            </article>

            <article>
                <h3>Deep Space Imagery</h3>
                <p>Modern observatories, such as the James Webb Space Telescope, capture infrared light from galaxies that formed over 13 billion years ago, near the beginning of time itself.</p>
            </article>
        </section>
